Research

AI and Data Science for Complex Intelligent Systems

We develop next-generation data science and artificial intelligence methods for modeling, understanding, and optimizing complex systems across healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, and smart infrastructure.

Overview

Research vision

The Data Science for Intelligent Systems (DSIS) Laboratory develops scalable, interpretable, and trustworthy AI algorithms that transform high-dimensional and multimodal data into actionable insights.

Our research lies at the intersection of Industrial & Systems Engineering, Machine Learning, Operations Research, and AI. We aim to bridge methodological innovation with real-world decision making by creating models that can support prediction, optimization, and intelligent system design.

Core Area

AI for complex systems

Modern engineering and societal systems continuously generate massive amounts of heterogeneous, high-dimensional data. Traditional statistical models often struggle to capture the rich interactions embedded within these datasets. Our research develops novel machine learning and data science methodologies that enable intelligent system modeling, prediction, optimization, and decision support.

Research Directions

Current research themes

High-Dimensional Data Analytics

We develop scalable statistical learning methods for high-dimensional structured data, including tensors, graphs, and multimodal datasets.

  • Tensor representation learning
  • Low-rank modeling
  • Sparse learning
  • Statistical inference
  • Representation learning

Tensor Analytics and Multimodal Learning

Many real-world systems naturally generate multiway data. We design tensor decomposition and tensor learning algorithms that preserve higher-order structures while enabling efficient downstream machine learning tasks.

  • Coupled tensor decomposition
  • Federated tensor learning
  • Dynamic tensor factorization
  • Multimodal representation learning
  • Self-supervised tensor learning

AI for Healthcare

Healthcare data are increasingly multimodal, combining electronic health records, medical imaging, genomics, wearable sensors, and clinical notes. We develop trustworthy AI methods for clinical decision support.

  • Computational phenotyping
  • Disease subtyping
  • Clinical risk prediction
  • Precision medicine
  • Personalized treatment recommendation

Intelligent Manufacturing and Smart Operations

Industrial systems are becoming increasingly data-driven. We develop AI-enabled optimization and learning algorithms for intelligent operations and smart manufacturing systems.

  • Smart manufacturing
  • Scheduling and rescheduling
  • Quality monitoring
  • Predictive maintenance
  • Digital twins
  • Intelligent operations

AI for Transportation and Smart Infrastructure

Emerging transportation and infrastructure systems continuously collect large-scale spatiotemporal data. We investigate AI methods for system modeling, prediction, and urban-scale analytics.

  • Traffic prediction
  • Transportation system modeling
  • Spatiotemporal learning
  • Urban computing
  • Intelligent transportation systems
  • Infrastructure analytics

Responsible and Trustworthy AI

As AI becomes increasingly integrated into engineering decision making, interpretability and robustness become essential. We investigate AI methods that are reliable, privacy-aware, and human-centered.

  • Explainable AI
  • Robust machine learning
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Privacy-preserving learning
  • Federated learning
  • Human-centered AI
