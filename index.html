Data Science for Intelligent Systems Laboratory

AI and data science for complex intelligent systems

We develop next-generation data science and artificial intelligence methods for modeling, understanding, and optimizing complex systems across healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, and smart infrastructure.

Explore Research Join the Group

Research Vision

The DSIS Laboratory bridges methodological innovation with real-world decision making by developing scalable, interpretable, and trustworthy AI algorithms that transform high-dimensional and multimodal data into actionable insights.

Welcome

Welcome to the DSIS Laboratory

The Data Science for Intelligent Systems Laboratory is led by Dr. Meng Zhao in the Department of Industrial & Systems Engineering at Lehigh University.

Our research lies at the intersection of Industrial & Systems Engineering, Machine Learning, Operations Research, and AI. We design methods that support intelligent system modeling, prediction, optimization, and decision support.

At a glance

  • AI, machine learning, and data science
  • Tensor analytics and multimodal learning
  • Healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation applications
  • Scalable, interpretable, and trustworthy decision support

Research

Core Research Themes

We develop AI and data science methods that turn heterogeneous, high-dimensional, and multimodal data into knowledge for complex engineering and societal systems.

01

AI for Complex Systems

Machine learning and data science methods for modeling, prediction, optimization, and decision support in complex systems.

02

High-Dimensional Data Analytics

Scalable statistical learning for tensors, graphs, structured data, and multimodal datasets.

03

Tensor & Multimodal Learning

Tensor decomposition and tensor learning algorithms that preserve higher-order structure for downstream machine learning.

04

AI for Healthcare

Trustworthy AI for computational phenotyping, disease subtyping, clinical risk prediction, precision medicine, and treatment recommendation.

05

Smart Operations & Infrastructure

AI-enabled learning and optimization for manufacturing, scheduling, predictive maintenance, transportation, and urban systems.

06

Responsible & Trustworthy AI

Explainable AI, robustness, uncertainty quantification, privacy-preserving learning, federated learning, and human-centered AI.
