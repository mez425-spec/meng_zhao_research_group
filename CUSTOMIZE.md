# Content Customisation Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing the website.

## Global replacement

Search and replace these placeholders in all `.html` files:

- `Research Group Name`
- `Department / School Name`
- `University / Institution Name`
- `group.email@example.edu`
- `group.lead@example.edu`
- `Building / Office / Campus Address`
- `Professor / Dr Full Name`

## Pages to edit

### `index.html`

- Homepage title
- Short mission statement
- At-a-glance card
- Research theme cards
- Latest news

### `research.html`

- Research overview
- Research themes
- Methods and tools

### `people.html`

- Group lead profile
- Current members
- Alumni table

### `publications.html`

- Paper titles
- Authors
- Journal/conference names
- Paper/code/evidenceset links

### `projects.html`

- Funded projects
- Student projects
- Software and resources

### `news.html`

- Publication announcements
- Funding news
- Student awards
- Talks and workshops

### `openings.html`

- PhD opportunities
- RA/intern positions
- Required skills
- Contact instructions

### `contact.html`

- Email
- Office address
- Social links
- Map placeholder

## Images

Replace files in `assets/img/` or update image paths in HTMD:

- `hero-network.svg`
- `group-photo-placeholder.svg`
- `avatar-lead.svg`
- `avatar-student.svg`
- `avatar-researcher.svg`
